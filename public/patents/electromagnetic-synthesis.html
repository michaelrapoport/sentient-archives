INTEGRATED SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR ADVANCED ELECTROMAGNETIC FIELD MANIPULATION, SEMICONDUCTOR SWITCHING, AND MATERIAL SYNTHESIS CONTROL

ABSTRACT

[0001] A multi-domain system for high-precision electromagnetic control and advanced material synthesis is disclosed. The system includes an interference-based masking apparatus for wireless power transfer, utilizing a spatial interference array (104) to generate a phase-inverted field to suppress eddy currents in foreign objects. A hybrid saturation-graded mask architecture utilizes a Vanadium-Permendur core (102) and Mu-Metal cladding (104) for high-intensity flux focusing. The system further incorporates high-speed Silicon Carbide (SiC) power modules with integrated active quenching and bufferless resonance kernel processors for cryogenic signal processing. Additionally, a eutectic adamantane-phthalonitrile resin blend and a biphasic dendrite-suppressing battery interface provide enhanced structural and electrochemical stability.

FIELD OF THE INVENTION

[0002] The present invention relates generally to electromagnetic systems and materials science. More specifically, the invention relates to interference-based masking for wireless power transfer, high-saturation magnetic architectures, high-temperature thermosetting resins, fast-quench power semiconductors, and precise valence control in epitaxial synthesis.

BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

[0003] Conventional wireless power transfer (WPT) systems suffer from parasitic heating when foreign object debris (FOD) is present within the primary magnetic field. Standard mitigation techniques involve system shutdown, which interrupts power delivery. Furthermore, in magnetic flux manipulation, standard materials reach magnetic saturation at approximately 0.8 Tesla, limiting the precision of high-intensity field focusing.

[0004] In the realm of power electronics, Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFETs offer high switching speeds but are susceptible to extreme inductive flyback energy, requiring dissipation within nanosecond timeframes. Concurrently, in battery technology, the growth of lithium dendrites remains a critical failure mode for high-energy-density cells, necessitating interfaces that satisfy the Monroe-Newman criterion without sacrificing ionic conductivity.

SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

[0005] To address these deficiencies, the present invention provides a unified architectural approach to electromagnetic and material control. A first aspect involves an interference-based masking manifold configured to project a spatially-conformal masking envelope (110) around a detected object (106) using a micro-coil array (104).

[0006] A second aspect includes a hybrid saturation-graded mask comprising a high-saturation core (102) and a high-permeability cladding (104) capable of handling flux densities exceeding 3.0 Tesla.

[0007] A third aspect involves a fast-quench SiC power module utilizing an active clamping mechanism and Aluminum Nitride (AlN) substrates to achieve quench times below 50 nanoseconds.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE PREFERRED EMBODIMENTS

[0008] Referring to FIG. 1, the system for interference-based masking of foreign object debris (FOD) comprises a primary field generator 102 operating at 80-150 kHz. A processing manifold 114, preferably implemented as an FPGA, receives signals from a sensor array 112 to identify the electromagnetic signature of an object 106. The manifold 114 commands a high-resolution spatial array 104 to generate a phase-inverted field.

[0009] The total magnetic flux density \( B_{total}(r) \) is given by the relation:

\[ B_{total}(r) = B_p + B_i(r) \]

wherein \( B_i \approx -B_p \) within the volume of the FOD 106, effectively suppressing eddy current density according to:

\[ \nabla^2 A + \sigma \left( \frac{\partial A}{\partial t} + \nabla \phi \right) = 0 \]

[0010] In another embodiment, a hybrid saturation-graded mask architecture is provided. The mask comprises a core 102 of Vanadium-Permendur (48-50% Co, 48-50% Fe, 2% V) and a cladding 104 of Mu-Metal. This allows for flux handling exceeding 3.0 Tesla. The Flux Leakage Ratio (FLR) is maintained below 0.05, calculated as:

\[ FLR = \frac{\Phi_{leak}}{\Phi_{total}} < 0.05 \]

[0011] For power semiconductor applications, the system includes a fast-quench SiC module. The module utilizes pressure-assisted silver sintering at 106 to attach the die to an Aluminum Nitride (AlN) substrate 102. The active clamp trigger responds to inductive flyback energy:

\[ V_{DS}(t) = (L \times \frac{dI}{dt}) + V_{residual} \]

The system ensures a quench time \( T_{quench} < 50 \) ns, significantly reducing the risk of avalanche breakdown.

[0012] Regarding advanced materials, a low-melt adamantane-phthalonitrile blend is disclosed. The blend utilizes a eutectic ratio of approximately 50:50 weight percent between ethynyl-substituted adamantane and bis(phthalonitrile). The melting point \( T_m \) is suppressed to 90-110°C, enabling processing with standard aerospace equipment while maintaining a glass transition temperature \( T_g > 500 \text{°C} \).

[0013] For signal processing in extreme environments, a bufferless resonance kernel processor (RKP) is utilized. The RKP eliminates pipeline buffers to reduce thermal jitter. It implements a Phase-Gradient Vector Projection (PGVP) for real-time correction:

\[ \theta_{new} = \theta_{old} + \eta(R \odot S^*) \]

where \( R \) is the residue vector and \( S^* \) is the conjugate state vector.

CLAIMS

1. A system for mitigating foreign object debris (FOD) in wireless power transfer, the system comprising:
  • a primary field generator configured to generate a primary magnetic field;
  • a sensor array configured to detect an electromagnetic signature and a geometric profile of an object within the primary magnetic field;
  • a high-resolution spatial interference array comprising a plurality of micro-coils; and
  • a processing manifold configured to generate a control signal for the spatial interference array to produce a phase-inverted field, thereby creating a spatially-conformal masking envelope around the object.
2. The system of claim 1, wherein the processing manifold comprises an FPGA-based feedback loop with a latency of less than 10 microseconds.
3. A hybrid saturation-graded magnetic mask architecture comprising:
  • a core comprising Vanadium-Permendur with a saturation induction of approximately 2.4 Tesla; and
  • a high-permeability cladding comprising Mu-Metal;
  • wherein the architecture is configured to maintain a flux leakage ratio of less than 0.05 at field intensities exceeding 3.0 Tesla.
4. A fast-quench power semiconductor module comprising:
  • a Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFET;
  • an Aluminum Nitride (AlN) Direct Bonded Copper (DBC) substrate; and
  • an integrated active quenching circuit configured to dissipate inductive flyback energy in less than 50 nanoseconds.
5. A thermosetting resin composition comprising a eutectic blend of:
  • ethynyl-substituted adamantane monomers; and
  • bis(phthalonitrile) monomers;
  • wherein the weight ratio of adamantane to phthalonitrile is between 48:52 and 52:48, resulting in a melting point between 90°C and 110°C.
6. A bufferless processor architecture for cryogenic signal processing, comprising:
  • a recursive datapath comprising a hardware multiplier array and a multi-operand adder tree; and
  • a phase-gradient vector projection (PGVP) unit configured to extract residue vectors and generate real-time correction signals;
  • wherein the datapath is substantially free of pipeline buffers to eliminate thermal jitter.
