Claims

1. A system for active decoherence suppression in a quantum channel, the system comprising: an array of hybrid nanothermometers configured to generate a real-time, high-resolution spatial map of local thermal and magnetic noise; a high-speed controller configured to receive the spatial map and calculate corrective optical field parameters; and a spatial light modulator responsive to the high-speed controller and configured to apply spatially-targeted optical fields to the quantum channel to actively counteract the local thermal and magnetic noise. 2. The system of claim 1, wherein the spatial light modulator is configured to operate at a modulation frequency of at least 10 MHz. 3. The system of claim 1, wherein the spatially-targeted optical fields are configured to create an actively stabilized quiet zone characterized by a reduction in decoherence rates relative to an unmodulated environment. 4. The system of claim 1, wherein the array of hybrid nanothermometers comprises a plurality of quantum sensors integrated with a thermal transducer to provide concurrent readout of magnetic field fluctuations and temperature gradients. 5. The system of claim 1, wherein the high-speed controller utilizes a feed-forward control algorithm to adjust the spatial light modulator at a rate exceeding the characteristic timescale of the detected local noise.
