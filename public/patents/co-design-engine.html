Multi-Physics Generative Co-Design Engine

Abstract

A system for An AI engine that simultaneously optimizes physical geometries and active control systems for a broad range of complex multi-physics problems beyond phononics.

Claims

1. A system for multi-physics generative co-design, comprising: a digital memory storing a multi-physics simulation model; and a processor configured to execute a generative engine that simultaneously optimizes a physical geometry and a control logic for an active control system, wherein the generative engine performs concurrent updates to parameters of the physical geometry and control gains of the control logic to achieve a target performance metric evaluated via the multi-physics simulation model. 2. The system of claim 1, wherein the generative engine comprises a reinforcement learning agent configured to explore a joint design space comprising structural dimensions of the physical geometry and temporal parameters of the control logic. 3. The system of claim 1, wherein the multi-physics simulation model comprises a coupled evaluation of at least two domains selected from the group consisting of: structural mechanics, fluid dynamics, electromagnetics, and thermodynamics. 4. The system of claim 1, wherein the active control system comprises a defined arrangement of sensors and actuators, and wherein the generative engine is further configured to optimize the spatial placement of the sensors and actuators relative to the physical geometry. 5. The system of claim 1, wherein the generative engine utilizes a neural network trained on a synthetic dataset generated by iterative physics-based simulations to predict performance gradients for both the physical geometry and the control logic.

Description

An AI engine that simultaneously optimizes physical geometries and active control systems for a broad range of complex multi-physics problems beyond phononics.
