Adaptive Countermeasure System for Quantum Jamming

Inventor: Michael Rapoport from Polaritronics Inc.

Abstract

A system for Uses the classification engine to identify deliberate hostile jamming signatures and applies tailored physical-layer countermeasures to harden military quantum sensors and radar.

Claims

1. An adaptive countermeasure system for quantum sensors, the system comprising: a classification engine configured to receive signal data from a quantum sensor and identify, within the signal data, a jamming signature corresponding to a deliberate hostile interference; and a controller operatively coupled to the classification engine, the controller configured to select a tailored physical-layer countermeasure based on the identified jamming signature and apply the tailored physical-layer countermeasure to the quantum sensor to mitigate the deliberate hostile interference. 2. The adaptive countermeasure system of claim 1, wherein the identification of the jamming signature comprises distinguishing between stochastic environmental decoherence and coherent state manipulation by an external source. 3. The adaptive countermeasure system of claim 1, wherein the tailored physical-layer countermeasure comprises an automated adjustment of a local oscillator frequency or a temporal synchronization shift to avoid detection by the hostile interference. 4. The adaptive countermeasure system of claim 1, wherein the quantum sensor comprises an entanglement-based radar system, and the physical-layer countermeasure comprises a dynamic change in an entanglement purification protocol. 5. The adaptive countermeasure system of claim 1, further comprising a real-time feedback loop configured to measure a signal-to-noise ratio of the quantum sensor after the application of the tailored physical-layer countermeasure and iteratively update the classification engine via machine learning.
