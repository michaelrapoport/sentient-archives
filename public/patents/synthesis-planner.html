Multi-Objective Synthesis Planner with Pareto Front Visualization

Abstract

A system for An advanced user interface where users define multiple, often competing, objectives (e.g., maximize yield, minimize cost, minimize CO2 emissions, minimize reaction time). The AI synthesizer returns a Pareto front of optimal solutions, which the user can explore on an interactive 2D/3D scatter plot to visually understand the trade-offs and select a pathway that best fits their business and practical priorities. (Based on: Evolution of the 'Goal-Driven' feature of the Original Concept)

Claims

1. A computer-implemented system for multi-objective chemical synthesis planning, comprising: a user interface configured to receive a plurality of synthesis objectives including at least two competing metrics; a synthesis processing engine comprising an artificial intelligence model configured to generate a plurality of chemical synthesis pathways for a target molecule; an optimization module configured to identify a Pareto front of optimal pathways from the plurality of chemical synthesis pathways by evaluating each pathway against the plurality of synthesis objectives; and a visualization module configured to display the Pareto front on an interactive scatter plot, wherein the interactive scatter plot facilitates user selection of a specific synthesis pathway based on visual trade-off analysis. 2. The system of claim 1, wherein the plurality of synthesis objectives is selected from a group consisting of: chemical yield, monetary cost, carbon dioxide emissions, reaction time, and safety risk metrics. 3. The system of claim 1, wherein the visualization module is configured to render the Pareto front as a three-dimensional scatter plot, where each axis corresponds to a distinct synthesis objective from the plurality of synthesis objectives. 4. The system of claim 1, wherein the artificial intelligence model utilizes a multi-objective reinforcement learning algorithm or a genetic algorithm to navigate the chemical space and identify candidate pathways for the Pareto front. 5. The system of claim 1, further comprising a pathway execution module configured to transmit parameters of the user-selected synthesis pathway to an automated laboratory synthesis robot.
